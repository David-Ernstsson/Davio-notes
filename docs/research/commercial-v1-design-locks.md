## 1. Phone web versus an installed Android app

Gym-era [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-responsive-web-v1.md) (first commit 2026-08-21, current text 2026-08-22) owns the client shape for the Gym: v1 is **one responsive web app**; Expo/React Native was a preference, not a requirement; a native phone app is later, after the Core loop is proven. [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-mobile-first-phone.md) (2026-08-22) owns the sitting: phone, not desk; still that same web app; desk, if it happens, is the phone layout stretched. The [prototype skill](../../.agents/skills/prototype/SKILL.md) still cites ADR-0003 for phone-first mockups.

Later, 2026-09-08, the owner mandate and V1 foundation bind commercial launch to Android/Play without naming a native runtime. [product-track-mandate.md](../product-experience/product-track-mandate.md) **Owner-resolved starting premises**: “The first real release is a quiet public German V1 for Android through Google Play.” A small required Play test group validates installation, purchasing, and catastrophic failures; reusing `com.davio` is a later deployment choice. [v1-product-foundation.md](../product-experience/v1-product-foundation.md) (status 2026-09-08; Practice lock 2026-09-16): “German launches first on Android through Google Play”; the store title may be **Davio: Learn German**; launch uses the Play test group, then a quiet public German V1; ratings use Google’s in-app review surface. **Deliberately deferred** there: whether to update the existing `com.davio` Google Play product in place or use another delivery path, after the product and technical shape are known.

Those later documents are the commercial-V1 launch lock. They contradict ADR-0001’s “web now, native later” if “Android through Google Play” is read as “installed native app first.” They do **not** actually say native. They lock **store and device class**. ADR-0001 still owns the only named client technology (one responsive web app). The mandate also says to keep existing ADRs until an evidence-based audit says otherwise ([product-track-mandate.md](../product-experience/product-track-mandate.md) How to work), so the collision is recorded, not silently repealed.

[GitHub issue 7](https://github.com/David-Ernstsson/Davio/issues/7) (Davio 2 commercial fidelity) is the throwaway Swedish first-session demo, not the commercial V1 client choice. Its body: cheap stays cheap — no Play packaging inside that map; “Looks like the end product ≠ production architecture, accounts, billing, or Play packaging. Fidelity is learner-facing; platform stays deferred.” Out of scope there: shipping the Google Play binary; the `com.davio` reuse decision.

**Still open for commercial V1:** phone browser versus Play-wrapped web versus a new installed Android app as the first real client; `com.davio` versus another listing.

## 2. When implementation is allowed to start

[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) **Do 4** owns the learner-surface gate: a new or reshaped learner page, flow, or primary layout goes through the prototype skill’s mockups, **stops for approval**, then implements. Button moves, copy, bugfixes, and wiring an approved layout skip both prototype and ux-design.

The [prototype skill](../../.agents/skills/prototype/SKILL.md) owns the start condition: mockup pictures in the owner’s hands **end the look-and-feel turn**; do not implement production UI in the same turn; a playable HTML prototype is a second step only if asked; **implementation starts only after an explicit go, and is a rewrite**, not promotion of mockup pixels or prototype HTML. Wayfinder maps that include a new learner surface keep a prototype ticket blocking implementation. [ux-design](../../.agents/skills/ux-design/SKILL.md) hands a new or reshaped surface to that same prototype stop.

The mandate’s **Delivery sequence** ([product-track-mandate.md](../product-experience/product-track-mandate.md)) is later than the Gym ADRs: publishing seam → audit → Product Foundation → explicit evolve/renovate/rebuild → design and test vertical learner experiences → **then** derive implementation. The Product Foundation is approved; it is “not a screen specification, implementation plan” ([v1-product-foundation.md](../product-experience/v1-product-foundation.md)).

Issue 7’s **Decisions so far** point at the demo surface and stop, not a commercial rewrite: new design-led pile, not renovate `v1-vertical` / `world-proto` ([04-evolve-vs-new-surface.md](../../.scratch/davio-2-commercial-fidelity/issues/04-evolve-vs-new-surface.md)); Atlas spike at `/?pile=atlas` ([05-editorial-atlas-spike.md](../../.scratch/davio-2-commercial-fidelity/issues/05-editorial-atlas-spike.md)); Atlas slice failed the learner session — next is critique and redesign, **no implementation**, do not patch the walker ([issue 7](https://github.com/David-Ernstsson/Davio/issues/7)). Swedish Activity close: prototype done, implementation remains separate ([issue 7](https://github.com/David-Ernstsson/Davio/issues/7); [swedish-activity-close-decision.md](../product-experience/swedish-activity-close-decision.md) 2026-09-16). Explore structure: “Prototype only, no production implementation”; approving Explore alone does not authorize implementation ([swedish-explore-screen-decision.md](../product-experience/swedish-explore-screen-decision.md)).

**Still open:** the explicit go that starts **production** of a commercial-feeling German V1 (as opposed to demo piles and screen tickets); whether that rewrite is into the current web client or a new production client.

## 3. Who owns tokens, UX, and visual design — and what the founder approves

[product-track-mandate.md](../product-experience/product-track-mandate.md) **Operating model** (2026-09-08) owns the split for the product track: owner = vision, taste, priorities, business intent, judgement on consequential choices; AI team = professional product, learning-design, UX, brand, engineering, QA, accessibility, growth, analytics, and research beneath that level. The team researches and recommends; it does not ask the owner to invent specialist solutions; detailed product-team and engineering decisions are autonomous.

**Product decision levels** in that same file:

- **Owner:** positioning, business model, major navigation and motivation mechanics, consequential progression philosophy, privacy/data policy, significant brand direction, and the role of Stories/Characters. The team recommends one for approval.
- **Product-team:** detailed recall scheduling, unlock thresholds, information hierarchy, interaction behaviour, accessibility, empty/loading/error states, animation, and detailed UX. The team decides and records why.
- **Engineering:** module seams, indexes, caching, pagination, state management, and test organization — autonomous.

That is the commercial-track contract for who owns **design tokens** (product-team detailed UX), **UX** (screens via the team; major navigation via the founder), and **visual / brand** (significant brand direction is an owner decision; application is team work). [ux-design](../../.agents/skills/ux-design/SKILL.md) is the method for learner screens, sitting chrome, and phone navigation; [prototype](../../.agents/skills/prototype/SKILL.md) is when the founder sees stills.

Issue 7 records a **demo-only** founder-gate list, pointing at [03-ux-design-ownership.md](../../.scratch/davio-2-commercial-fidelity/issues/03-ux-design-ownership.md) (founder 2026-09-09, option A): founder personally taste-gates only (1) first Explore viewport, (2) Activity play surface, (3) Result/unlock; at each gate, accept or change-list, not redesign-from-scratch. The AI design team owns tokens, type, spacing, motion, empty/error states, Profile/Stories chrome, and iteration between those gates. Issue 7’s body repeats that operating model and says “experts in UX and design” means the AI team drives and recommends; the founder remains approver of consequential taste. That three-gate list is the commercial-fidelity demo contract. No later owner foundation re-locks those three named screens as the commercial V1 approval set.

What the founder has already approved on the product track, aside from that demo list: German-first Android/Play launch, Explore / Stories / Profile, the V1 offer, Companion / Neck Wrap plus recommended restraint, and (as of 2026-09-13) that **selecting** visual territory remains core product-design work before a production visual system ([v1-product-foundation.md](../product-experience/v1-product-foundation.md); [brand-world.md](../product-experience/brand-world.md)).

**Still open:** whether commercial V1 uses issue 7’s three named gates or the mandate’s broader owner/team split only; where production tokens live and in what form.
