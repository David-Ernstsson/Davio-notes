# What B1–B2 German reading and listening can a solo hobby ship?

**Ticket:** [08](../../.scratch/discovery/issues/08-b1-b2-content-sources.md)  
**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Scope:** Curated German in the B1–B2 Study band (not A1–A2, not native-only). Reading and listening. Grammar via explanations and patterns in real text, not drills. Mixed news-ish and life-ish adult topics (not work-ish). One responsive web app. Hobby now; licensing must survive later commercialisation. The Learner’s own emails and podcasts are out of v1.

This note is research, not legal advice. Prefer first-party license pages, institute sites, publisher terms, and German statute. Every factual claim below is cited.

## How this note judges sources

Five axes, matching the ticket:

| Axis | Question for Davio |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | Can a hobby host the text/audio *and* still commercialise later without a rewrite? |
| **Burden** | How much selection, adaptation, and review does one owner need? |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Does the source sit in the Study band, or below (A1–A2 / Leichte Sprache) or above (native news)? |
| **Noticing / Grammar-in-context** | Do sentences carry real German patterns (cases, subordinate clauses, particles), or is syntax flattened? |
| **Desk vs phone** | Does a sitting of 20–40 min (desk) and 5–15 min (phone) both work on one responsive page? |

**CEFR anchors used below.** B1 reception: the Learner “can understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, leisure, etc.” and “can understand the main points of news bulletins and simpler recorded material about familiar subjects.” B2 reception: the Learner “can understand the main ideas of propositionally and linguistically complex discourse on both concrete and abstract topics.” ([Council of Europe, *CEFR Companion Volume*, 2020](https://rm.coe.int/common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching/16809ea0d4))

**Einfache vs Leichte Sprache.** Official German usage treats these as different bands. Leichte Sprache “folgt klaren Regeln und ist stärker vereinfacht als Einfache Sprache und Alltagssprache” ([BMAS](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Soziales/Teilhabe-und-Inklusion/Leichte-Sprache-anwenden/leichte-sprache-anwenden.html)). ARD: Einfache Sprache is “eine sprachlich vereinfachte Version von Standardsprache”; Leichte Sprache “geht noch weiter” and follows a fixed rule set ([ARD Hilfe](https://hilfe.ard.de/fernsehen/barrierefreie-angebote/einfache-und-leichte-sprache/)). A Baden-Württemberg state project maps Leichte Sprache to A1–A2 and Einfache Sprache to B1 ([Leichte Sprache BW FAQ](https://leichtesprache-bw.de/start/fragen-antworten)). That mapping is a planning heuristic, not a CEFR rating of any one article.

## Legal frame in Germany (the commercialisation test)

Davio lives in Germany. Default copyright term is 70 years after the author’s death ([§ 64 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html)). Only “persönliche geistige Schöpfungen” are works ([§ 2 Abs. 2 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__2.html)).

**Official works** (laws, ordinances, official decrees, court decisions, and other official works published for general notice) have no copyright, with source-citation and no-alteration rules for the second class ([§ 5 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__5.html)). Useful for rights, useless as Gym content: not news-ish/life-ish adult reading.

**Quotation** allows using as much of a published work as the quotation purpose justifies, when the quote supports the quoter’s own discussion ([§ 51 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__51.html)). That can back a Noticing explanation that cites a short sentence. It is not a licence to host a corpus of articles or podcasts.

**Classroom exception** allows limited, non-commercial use inside *Bildungseinrichtungen* for teachers and course participants ([§ 60a UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__60a.html)). A hobby web Gym for one Learner that may later take money is not that.

**Text and data mining** allows copies of lawfully accessible works for automated analysis unless the rightholder reserved the right in machine-readable form; copies must be deleted when no longer needed for mining ([§ 44b UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__44b.html)). Mining is not republication. Deutsche Welle separately forbids commercial TDM and AI-training use without written permission ([DW, *Freie Nutzung*](https://corporate.dw.com/de/freie-nutzung-von-dw-inhalten/a-76961025)).

**Creative Commons modules that matter for a later product:**

- **CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0:** share and adapt “for any purpose, even commercially,” subject to attribution and (for SA) share-alike ([CC BY-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en); [CC0 1.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en)).
- **NC:** “You may not use the material for commercial purposes.” Commercial use is “one primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation” ([CC BY-NC 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/deed.en)). A hobby that later charges, takes ads, or sells subscriptions fails NC.
- **ND:** no adaptations. Noticing notes *beside* an unaltered copy can be argued; rewriting the text for the Study band cannot.

**Implication:** do not build the Gym’s stored Activities on NC, ND, “private use only,” or “ask us first” sources. Link or embed those as optional companions. Own the corpus you intend to keep.

## 1. Public domain and official works

### What exists

- **Expired copyright (§ 64 UrhG):** German literary and journalistic works whose authors died in 1955 or earlier (term runs to the end of the calendar year; [§ 69 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__69.html) for computation). Typical hosts: German [Wikisource](https://de.wikisource.org/) (Wikimedia terms: text is CC BY-SA unless a page is public domain; commercial reuse of CC BY-SA is allowed if the licence is followed — [Wikimedia Terms of Use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use)); older scans on archive projects.
- **Project Gutenberg:** many German titles. The standard header says a work may be reused under the Project Gutenberg Licence in the United States, and “If you are not located in the United States, you will have to check the laws of the country where you are located before using this eBook” ([Project Gutenberg ebook header, typical wording](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/33460/pg33460.html)). US public domain ≠ German public domain.
- **LibriVox:** volunteer readings of texts that are public domain *in the United States*. Recordings are released into the public domain and “anyone can use all our recordings however they wish (even to sell them)” in the US; listeners outside the US must check local status ([LibriVox wiki, *Copyright and Public Domain*](https://wiki.librivox.org/index.php/Copyright_and_Public_Domain)).
- **§ 5 official works:** Gesetze, Urteile, amtliche Bekanntmachungen — free to reuse with the citation/no-alteration limits above.

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | Clean *if* the work is public domain in Germany, or is a § 5 official work. Gutenberg/LibriVox US status is not enough. A modernised spelling or abridgement can itself be a new work ([§ 2 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__2.html)); the underlying public-domain text stays free. |
| **Burden** | High selection. Most PD German is 19th/early-20th-century literature or officialese, not 2020s life-ish prose. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Usually too hard or too archaic. CEFR B1 expects “clear standard language” on familiar matters ([CEFR Companion Volume](https://rm.coe.int/common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching/16809ea0d4)); Fontane or a Reichsgericht opinion is not that. Short, modernised excerpts can be forced into band after review. |
| **Noticing** | Rich morphology and clause structure — often *too* rich, plus obsolete lexicon. Poor default for “why *dem*?” in today’s German. |
| **Desk vs phone** | Long texts are desk-only. Phone needs cut excerpts (200–400 words) plus optional audio (LibriVox quality varies by volunteer). |

**v1 role:** occasional excerpt after the owner checks German PD status and modernises. Not a feed.

## 2. Creative Commons and OER

### What exists

**Bundeszentrale für politische Bildung (bpb).** Most site texts carry a CC licence at the foot of the article. The most common licence is **CC BY-NC-ND** (no commercial use, no adaptation). A smaller OER set is **CC BY** or **CC BY-SA**. As of August 2022, bpb counted about 9,500 CC-licensed articles, of which about 1,200 articles, 500 videos, and 100 audios were usable as OER (changes and commercial use allowed). The site search can filter “Änderungen und kommerzielle Nutzung erlaubt (OER)” ([bpb, *Nutzungsrechte*](https://www.bpb.de/die-bpb/faq/184955/nutzungsrechte/)). Themenblätter from issue 122 are **CC BY-SA 4.0** ([bpb, *Die Themenblätter als OER*](https://www.bpb.de/shop/materialien/themenblaetter/311145/die-themenblaetter-als-oer/)). Pictures are often dpa/picture-alliance and *not* included in the text licence ([bpb, *Nutzungsrechte*](https://www.bpb.de/die-bpb/faq/184955/nutzungsrechte/)).

**Wikipedia / Wikivoyage / other Wikimedia text.** New edits are CC BY-SA 4.0; older edits remain BY-SA 3.0, which may be adapted under 4.0. Commercial reuse is allowed if attribution, share-alike, and change-marking are met ([Wikimedia Terms of Use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use); [Wikipedia:Copyrights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights); [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en)).

**Serlo.** Learning pages are **CC BY-SA 4.0**; commercial reuse is allowed with attribution and share-alike on the whole work that includes Serlo text ([serlo.org licence page](https://de.serlo.org/license/detail/1)). The catalogue is mostly school subjects, “die meisten Inhalte haben wir in Mathematik” ([Über Serlo](https://de.serlo.org/serlo)). Thin for adult news-ish/life-ish German.

**ZUM Deutsch Lernen** (Zentrale für Unterrichtsmedien). A DaF/DaZ teacher wiki. ZUM wikis default to **CC BY-SA 3.0 DE** unless a page says otherwise; some author pages remain non-commercial classroom-only ([ZUM, *20 Jahre ZUM.de und OER*](https://www.zum.de/portal/zumde-und-oer/); [ZUM-Wiki-Lizenz template](https://deutsch-lernen.zum.de/wiki/Vorlage:ZUM-Wiki-Lizenz)). Content is teacher-made: grammar boxes, Lernpfade, Übungen — mixed quality, often school-flavoured, must be checked page by page.

**Tatoeba.** Sentence dumps default to **CC BY 2.0 FR**; a subset is CC0 ([Tatoeba downloads](https://dev.tatoeba.org/en/downloads)). Isolated example sentences, not Activities. Quality is volunteer-uneven.

**Mozilla Common Voice (German).** Scripted-speech corpus **CC0-1.0**; intended for ASR and also “computer-aided language learning.” German 26.0: about 1,490 hours, 2.06 million sentences ([Mozilla Data Collective, Common Voice German 26.0](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmqim3xpi00t6nr07k0myqtkr)). Isolated read sentences, not curated articles. Speakers must not be re-identified.

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | **Keep:** CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, after per-item licence check (especially bpb and ZUM). **Do not store as the Gym corpus:** CC BY-NC / NC-ND (most of bpb). Share-alike is commercially legal but infects derivative pages that remix the text — Gym explanations *about* an SA article may need to be SA too if they are adaptations ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en)). |
| **Burden** | Medium–high. The commercial-OK slice is small and must be filtered. Wikipedia/Wikivoyage need heavy cutting to Study-band length. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | bpb civic essays and Wikipedia are usually native B2+ / C1. OER Themenblätter are classroom worksheets, not reading/listening Activities. Serlo is the wrong topic mix. Tatoeba/Common Voice are sentences, not texts. |
| **Noticing** | Native CC text (bpb OER, Wikipedia) has real grammar. School OER often *is* the drill the Gym rejected. |
| **Desk vs phone** | bpb/Wikipedia long reads are desk. Short OER worksheets and Tatoeba lines can be phone-sized but are the wrong Activity shape. |

**v1 role:** a **filter, not a feed**. Use bpb’s “OER / commercial use allowed” search and keep only short, adult, non-work pieces. Treat Wikipedia as a quote mine under § 51 plus SA if you adapt, not as the default article source.

## 3. Goethe-Institut, ÖSD, telc

These are the institutes the ticket named. They publish **exam models**, not an open content API.

### Goethe-Institut

Free **Modellsätze** and **Übungssätze** for Goethe-Zertifikat B1 and B2 (PDF + Hör-audio) sit on institute exam pages (example: [Übungsmaterialien B1, Goethe-Institut Deutschland](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/m/prf/prf/gzb1/ueb.html)). The B1 adult Modellsatz is © Goethe-Institut / ÖSD / Universität Freiburg and exists to simulate the exam ([B1 Modellsatz Erwachsene PDF](https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/materialien/B1/b1_modellsatz_erwachsene.pdf)).

Paid-course AGB: “Sämtliches Kurs- und Prüfungsmaterial … ist urheberrechtlich geschützt.” Learners get only a simple, non-transferable right for personal use in the course. Copying for third parties, putting material on the internet (paid or free), resale, and commercial use are forbidden ([Goethe-Institut AGB](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf356/agb_goethe-institut_ev_2022_de-v1.pdf)). Free website use is under separate *Nutzungsbedingungen* (read/publish on goethe.de), not a sublicence to host Goethe texts in another app ([example: Humboldt Nutzungsbedingungen](https://www.goethe.de/prj/hum/de/nut.html)).

**Deutsch für dich** still offers free A1–C2 exercises on Goethe’s site after the community login was shut down ([Deutsch für dich](https://www.goethe.de/prj/dfd/de/home.cfm)). That is a destination to *send* the Learner to, not a corpus to import.

### ÖSD

ÖSD publishes free **Modellsätze** (and some Übungssätze) on its [Downloads](https://www.osd.at/downloads/) page. The Prüfungsordnung points candidates to further “Übungsmaterialien mit weiteren Übungssätzen, die online bestellt werden können” ([ÖSD Prüfungsordnung, from 1 April 2026](https://www.osd.at/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/OSD-Prufungsordnung_gilt-ab_01-April-2026.pdf)). Actual exam papers are ÖSD property, copyright-protected, confidential, and “dürfen in keiner Form abgeändert oder vervielfältigt werden” (same Prüfungsordnung, § 6).

### telc

Official Übungstests for Deutsch B1 and B2 are free downloads from telc. The imprint: the publication and its parts are copyright-protected; any use beyond statutory exceptions needs the publisher’s written consent ([telc Deutsch B1 Übungstest 1](https://shop.telc.net/media/catalog/product/file/2/0/20231106_5060-b00-010102_web.pdf)).

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | **No** for hosting, remixing, or a later product. Personal exam prep on their PDFs is fine. Putting items in Davio is republication. |
| **Burden** | Low if you only *link*. High (and still unlawful) if you try to extract a corpus. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Best-in-class calibration. Adult B1/B2 exam texts are exactly the Study band. |
| **Noticing** | Reading and listening items are real-ish texts; many tasks around them are drills (multiple choice, Sprachbausteine). The Gym can learn *shape* from them without copying items. |
| **Desk vs phone** | PDFs and 35–40 min Hör modules are desk/exam-shaped, not a 5–15 min phone Activity. |

**v1 role:** **calibration and link-out only.** Steal the *length and topic mix* of a B1 Lesetext, not the text.

## 4. Nachrichtenleicht, tagesschau, and other Einfache / Leichte Sprache news

### Nachrichtenleicht (Deutschlandfunk / Deutschlandradio)

Daily news in Einfache Sprache: Politik, Wirtschaft, Gesellschaft, Kultur, Sport. Articles have a **Hören** button and **Audio runterladen**. An RSS URL is published for personal readers: `https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/nachrichten-leicht-startseite-100.rss` ([nachrichtenleicht, *Wie benutze ich*](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/benutzung-100.html)). The site is “ein Angebot der Deutschlandfunk-Nachrichtenredaktion” ([Impressum](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/impressum-102.html)). Photos: users may use them only privately and may not republish or otherwise reuse them ([Impressum](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/impressum-102.html)).

Deutschlandradio’s online terms (deutschlandfunk.de and sibling sites): contents are copyright-protected; even excerpt reproduction, storage, or republication needs **prior written consent**; use is “ausschließlich zu privaten, nichtkommerziellen Zwecken”; commercial use or any direct or indirect financial advantage (ads, sponsoring, subscription, etc.) is “stets unzulässig” ([Deutschlandfunk Nutzungsbedingungen](https://www.deutschlandfunk.de/nutzungsbedingungen-102.html)). nachrichtenleicht.de is the same newsroom’s product; treat those terms as the reuse rule unless DLR grants a separate licence.

### tagesschau in Einfacher Sprache (ARD-aktuell / NDR)

Weekday TV news rewritten in Einfache Sprache, slower speech, also on tagesschau.de, Mediathek, and YouTube ([tagesschau, 12 June 2024](https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/tagesschau-start-in-einfacher-sprache-100.html); [ARD Hilfe](https://hilfe.ard.de/fernsehen/barrierefreie-angebote/einfache-und-leichte-sprache/)). ARD Mediathek terms: all contents copyright-protected; reproduction or storage needs written consent of the producing Land broadcaster; use is private and non-commercial; the official embed player is limited to **private, non-commercial** sharing and must stay ad-free around the player ([ARD Mediathek Nutzungsbedingungen](https://www.ardmediathek.de/nutzungsbedingungen)).

Sister offerings (MDR/NDR/SWR/WDR Leichte or Einfache Sprache) sit under the same public-broadcasting copyright default ([ARD Hilfe list](https://hilfe.ard.de/fernsehen/barrierefreie-angebote/einfache-und-leichte-sprache/)).

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | **Hobby private listening/reading: yes. Hosting in Davio: no. Later commercial product: no** without a written DLR/ARD licence. Embed of tagesschau is also non-commercial-only. |
| **Burden** | Zero if the Learner opens their sites. Infinite if Davio copies them. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Einfache Sprache is the *lower* edge of the Study band (≈ B1 heuristic). Useful for news-ish topics; often too simple for B2. Leichte Sprache (MDR/NDR etc.) is A1–A2 and out of v1. |
| **Noticing** | Weak for the Grammar job. These texts are rewritten to drop complexity: short sentences, few subordinates, explained hard words ([ARD Hilfe](https://hilfe.ard.de/fernsehen/barrierefreie-angebote/einfache-und-leichte-sprache/); [nachrichtenleicht Wörter-Buch](https://www.nachrichtenleicht.de/benutzung-100.html)). Good for comprehension; poor for “why *dem*?” |
| **Desk vs phone** | Excellent on both: short articles + 2–5 min audio, already mobile. That is why they tempt — and why a copy would be a product built on someone else’s feed. |

**v1 role:** **companion tab**, not corpus. The Gym can deep-link a Friday piece. It must not ingest RSS into stored Activities.

## 5. “Slow German” podcasts (not public-broadcast news)

### Slow German (Annik Rubens / Larissa Vassilian)

Self-described “Podcast & Materialien zum Deutschlernen auf **B1-/B2-Niveau**”; free podcast plus paid premium PDFs ([slowgerman.com](https://slowgerman.com/)). Footer: all site content is her copyright; using texts or audio to make money (e.g. YouTube) is forbidden; copying or uploading elsewhere needs permission (`podcast@slowgerman.com`) ([slowgerman.com](https://slowgerman.com/)). Shop AGB name Larissa Vassilian as the seller ([Slow German AGB](https://slowgerman.com/en/agb/)).

Topics in the current episode list are life-ish / news-ish adult (Wehrpflicht, Social Media, Brustkrebs, ESC, Südtirol) — closer to Davio’s flavour than Nicos Weg’s job track ([slowgerman.com](https://slowgerman.com/)).

### News in Slow German (Linguistica 360) — a different product

US site. Content “may be used solely for your own personal, non-commercial purposes”; no copy, modify, or distribute without written permission; no automated scrape ([News in Slow German, Terms of use](https://www.newsinslowgerman.com/terms-of-use)).

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | **No** for hosting. A written one-off permission from Rubens could cover a hobby; it would still block commercialisation unless the contract says so. News in Slow German is closed. |
| **Burden** | Link-out is cheap. A licence negotiation is a human task. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Slow German is one of the few sources that *claims* B1/B2 and sounds like it. News in Slow German is pedagogically similar and contractually unusable. |
| **Noticing** | Strong: slow, clear, full sentences with adult syntax; transcripts exist. Premium worksheets are drill-shaped and paid. |
| **Desk vs phone** | Podcast-native: phone first, desk with transcript. |

**v1 role:** optional **outbound** listen. Do not scrape. If the owner wants it *inside* the Gym, email for a licence *before* writing a product that depends on it.

## 6. Deutsche Welle

### What exists (first-party catalogue)

DW’s Learn German hub labels levels on each format ([learngerman.dw.com Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/learn-german/s-9528); [Deutsch lernen mit Nachrichten](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen-mit-nachrichten/s-66405122)):

| Format | DW’s stated level | Fit for Davio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Top-Thema mit Vokabeln](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/top-thema/s-55861562) | **B1** — weekly short articles, audio, vocab/grammar exercises | News-ish adult; closest DW *reading* match to the Study band |
| [Langsam Gesprochene Nachrichten](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/langsam-gesprochene-nachrichten/s-60040332) | **B2** — Mon–Sat, slow + original-tempo MP3, full manuscript | News-ish listening; DW says these are “authentische und unveränderte Nachrichtentexte” ([format page](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/langsam-gesprochene-nachrichten/a-61694712)) — i.e. native news, slowed, not rewritten to B1 |
| [Video-Thema](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/learn-german-with-videos/s-68307454) | **B2** — short video reports, politics/culture/society | News-ish; video-heavy |
| [Das Bandtagebuch mit EINSHOCH6](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/das-bandtagebuch-mit-einshoch6-im-daf-unterricht/a-68456223) | **B2** (some clips A2/B1); everyday language, young-learner slant | Life-ish; younger than the owner’s adult mix |
| [Nicos Weg](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/nicos-weg/a-61693467) | **A1–B1**; “Leben und Arbeiten”; B1 “besonderen Fokus auf die Welt der Arbeit” | **Out:** beginner tree + work-ish |
| [Kurz und leicht](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/learn-german/s-9528) | **A2** video news | **Out:** below Study band |
| [Profis gesucht – Deutsch im Job](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/learn-german/s-9528) | **B1** job German | **Out:** work-ish |

### Rights (first-party)

DW content is copyright-protected. Without a licence, allowed uses are narrow and require **no alteration** plus source credit ([DW, *Freie Nutzung von DW-Inhalten*, 6 May 2026](https://corporate.dw.com/de/freie-nutzung-von-dw-inhalten/a-76961025)):

- **Link or official embed** — content stays on DW’s servers.
- **Private use** — own use, not published on a website or social network, not commercial, informal showing capped at 40 people.
- **Quotation** — only as much as needed, for the quoter’s own argument, not for decoration or entertainment.
- **Bildung & Wissenschaft** — concrete teaching/research purpose; access limited to pupils, students, or researchers; only as much as needed; **not** commercial (explicitly not “Lehrmaterialien, die zum Kauf oder werbefinanziert im Internet angeboten werden”).
- **TDM** — scientific research on freely accessible content; copies not republished; **commercial TDM and AI training require prior written permission**.

Any other use (including “Veröffentlichungen auf Webseiten”) goes through DW’s licensing form ([DW, *Lizenzierung*](https://corporate.dw.com/de/lizenzierung-von-dw-inhalten/a-76961136)).

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | **Embed/link in a private hobby: plausible. Store manuscripts/MP3s in Davio: no. Ship a commercial Gym on DW files: no** unless DW grants a deal. A later paywall or ad-funded site fails both “private” and “Bildung” tests. |
| **Burden** | Embed is cheap and brittle (DW can pull the embed). A licence is a human, slow process ([DW warns of backlog](https://corporate.dw.com/de/lizenzierung-von-dw-inhalten/a-76961136)). |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Top-Thema is the honest B1 reading source. LGN is labelled B2 but is *slowed native news* — the owner already rejected native news as the Study band. Good stretch listening; not the default Gym text. |
| **Noticing** | Top-Thema and LGN manuscripts have real syntax. DW’s own interactive exercises are often drill-shaped; Davio would want the *text*, which is exactly what the licence withholds. Embed-only is weak for sentence-level Noticing (hard to highlight *dem* inside a third-party player). |
| **Desk vs phone** | DW’s own pages are already responsive. LGN is ~7–9 min slow / ~5 min original ([LGN im DaF-Unterricht](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/die-langsam-gesprochenen-nachrichten-im-daf-unterricht/a-67714129)) — phone-possible. Top-Thema is a short article + audio — both form factors. |

**v1 role:** **best outbound news-ish companion**, not the stored corpus. If commercialisation becomes real, *ask DW first*; do not assume a hobby embed survives the pivot.

## 7. Publisher products and “APIs”

Hueber, Klett, and Cornelsen sell the B1–B2 textbooks everyone already knows (*Aspekte*, *Erkundungen*, *Studio*, etc.). They do **not** offer a public content API that lets a third-party app republish texts.

- **Klett:** digital licences are non-transferable; private or school-internal use; the customer “wird … nicht zur Weiterverbreitung der digitalen Inhalte oder zu sonstigen Zwecken nutzen” ([Klett, Nutzungsbedingungen für digitale Inhalte, 10 April 2026](https://www.klett.de/inhalt/sixcms/media.php/225/Klett_Nutzungsbedingungen_20260410.pdf)).
- **Hueber:** digital copies and uploads to a learning platform are “grundsätzlich nicht gestattet”; a platform deal needs a prior Nutzungsvereinbarung; **linking** to Hueber’s freely accessible, non-password pages is allowed ([Hueber, *Kopierregeln – Digitaler Unterricht*](https://www.hueber.de/en/ueber-uns/kopierregeln-digitaler-unterricht)).
- **Cornelsen:** digital media may be used only as the product licence says; typical classroom use requires a licence for every learner, class-only access, no pass-along to other schools ([Cornelsen, *FAQ Nutzung digitale Inhalte*](https://www.cornelsen.de/service/kundenservice/rechtliche-hinweise/faq-nutzung-digitale-inhalte)).

News-aggregator APIs (NewsAPI and similar) deliver headlines; they do **not** grant the newspaper’s copyright. That is a fetch pipe, not a source.

### Judgement

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Rights** | **No** for v1 hosting. A commercial publisher licence is a later, paid, negotiated product — not a hobby weekend. |
| **Burden** | Buying a personal ebook for the *owner* to study is legal and useless as Gym content. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Highest editorial quality in the band — and the wrong licence. |
| **Noticing** | Coursebooks mix authentic-ish texts with drills. Davio rejected the drill half. |
| **Desk vs phone** | Publisher apps exist; they are not Davio. |

**v1 role:** none as a pipeline. Link to a Hueber sample page if useful. Do not scan books.

## 8. Generate-and-review (original + optional model draft)

This is the only class that is **sized for a solo hobby** and **survives commercialisation**, if the owner treats the model as a typist, not as the author.

### Rights

- A fully model-generated text with no human creative stamp is **not** protected as a work. The German Patent and Trade Mark Office: “Ein von einer KI erstellter Inhalt … ist nicht urheberrechtlich geschützt, wenn er vollständig durch eine KI erzeugt wurde.” If the model is only a tool, it is case-by-case ([DPMA, *Geistiges Eigentum digital*, 20 March 2026](https://www.dpma.de/service/schutzrechte_kurz_erklaert/geistigeseigentumdigital/index.html)).
- German copyright still requires a **persönliche geistige Schöpfung** ([§ 2 Abs. 2 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__2.html)). A 2026 commentary decision in the BeckRS series: protection is conceivable only if human creative influence dominates the output so the result is the prompter’s own original creation ([Bayern.Recht / BeckRS 2026, 1513](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Pdf/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2026-N-1513?all=False)).
- **Usable ≠ exclusively yours.** Unprotected raw output can still be used; competitors can use the same idea. Substantial human rewrite (topic choice, structure, idiom, Grammar targets, fact check) is what makes a later product defensible.
- Output must not reproduce a recognisable pre-existing work. The Federal Ministry of Justice’s FAQ: if protected works remain recognisable in the result, you need the rightholder; if they are no longer recognisable, the result can be used ([BMJ, *Künstliche Intelligenz und Urheberrecht*](https://proqua-kms.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/240305_Bundesministerium-der-Justiz_FAQ_KI_Urheberrecht.pdf) — ministry FAQ text as republished).
- **Vendor terms** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, etc.) are a second layer and change; check the current commercial-use clause before a pivot. This note does not snapshot them.
- **Audio** is a separate licence: Azure/Neural TTS, ElevenLabs, and similar sell commercial voice use. Common Voice CC0 is for *training*, not a ready Activity soundtrack ([Common Voice German datasheet](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmqim3xpi00t6nr07k0myqtkr)).
- **Do not** feed DW, ARD, or DLR text into a model to “make it Davio’s.” DW forbids commercial TDM/AI training without written permission ([DW *Freie Nutzung*](https://corporate.dw.com/de/freie-nutzung-von-dw-inhalten/a-76961025)).

### Burden, quality, Noticing, form factor

| Axis | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Burden** | The real cost. A two-week MVP needs on the order of **10–20** short pieces (≈200–400 words + 2–4 min audio + a Noticing note), not a newsroom. Review must catch invented facts, Anglicisms, and wrong article/case. |
| **Quality at B1–B2** | Controllable. Prompt and edit to CEFR B1 “clear standard language” or B2 “linguistically complex” ([CEFR Companion Volume](https://rm.coe.int/common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching/16809ea0d4)). This is the only class that can hit the band *on purpose*. |
| **Noticing** | Best fit for *owned* running German plus an on-the-spot explanation. Authoring since 2026-08-24: write the scene first, then attach a catalog Pattern that occurs (gym-copy). Picking the chip first and repeating the form to fill the piece produces drill-shaped German. |
| **Desk vs phone** | Full control. One length for phone; a longer cut of the same piece for desk. One responsive page. |

**v1 role:** **default corpus.** Owner-written, or model-drafted and owner-rewritten until it would stand as the owner’s text.

## Implications for Davio MVP

**Own the corpus.** v1 Activities should be pieces Davio can keep: owner-written or owner-rewritten texts, plus separately licensed TTS (or owner-read audio). That is the only path that is legal for a hobby *and* still open if the Gym later charges. Do not ingest DW, DLR, ARD, Slow German, exam PDFs, or publisher books into the product database.

**Use the public web as a companion, not as a pipeline.** Deep-link Top-Thema (B1) or nachrichtenleicht for “today’s news-ish extra.” That satisfies curiosity without taking a licence the hobby cannot get. Embed, if used at all, stays non-commercial and official; it will have to be ripped out on a commercial pivot ([DW *Freie Nutzung*](https://corporate.dw.com/de/freie-nutzung-von-dw-inhalten/a-76961025); [ARD Mediathek Nutzungsbedingungen](https://www.ardmediathek.de/nutzungsbedingungen)).

**Write to the band, do not borrow flattened news.** Einfache Sprache and Leichte Sprache exist for accessibility, not for Grammar-in-context ([BMAS](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Soziales/Teilhabe-und-Inklusion/Leichte-Sprache-anwenden/leichte-sprache-anwenden.html); [ARD Hilfe](https://hilfe.ard.de/fernsehen/barrierefreie-angebote/einfache-und-leichte-sprache/)). Seed facts and register from real pages; write an owned scene; then attach one catalog Pattern that is in the text ([gym-copy](../../.agents/skills/gym-copy/SKILL.md)). Aim at CEFR B1 “clear standard language” and occasional B2 complexity ([CEFR Companion Volume](https://rm.coe.int/common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching/16809ea0d4)), not at native Tagesschau and not at A2 video news.

**Live piles (2026-08-24).** Frankfurt, Gießen, and classic `PIECES` are internal test text. They are not the corpus for a public beta or other people. Agents replace them with gym-copy sittings before that, still under ADR-0002 (owned text, not a stored DW/ZUM feed). The founder is not the language-review queue.

**Size the first two weeks.** Ten to twenty phone-length pieces (200–400 words, 2–4 minutes of audio, one Noticing note) are enough to test “opens most days.” Desk can show a longer cut of the same piece. That is a writing task, not a crawler task.

**If a later product needs volume,** the honest options are: keep generating-and-reviewing; filter bpb/ZUM/Wikimedia under BY/SA; or **buy** a DW or publisher licence. Asking Slow German or DW *before* designing features around their files is cheaper than a rewrite. NC-licensed bpb articles and “private use only” broadcast news are a dead end for commercialisation ([CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/deed.en); [Deutschlandfunk Nutzungsbedingungen](https://www.deutschlandfunk.de/nutzungsbedingungen-102.html)).

**Exam institutes are a yardstick, not a library.** Goethe, ÖSD, and telc show what B1–B2 adult reading and listening *look like*. Their copyright lines forbid the rest ([Goethe AGB](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf356/agb_goethe-institut_ev_2022_de-v1.pdf); [telc B1 Übungstest imprint](https://shop.telc.net/media/catalog/product/file/2/0/20231106_5060-b00-010102_web.pdf); [ÖSD Prüfungsordnung](https://www.osd.at/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/OSD-Prufungsordnung_gilt-ab_01-April-2026.pdf)).
