# Recommend a core learner screen

Owner-locked 2026-09-21; revised 2026-09-26 for [issue #94](https://github.com/David-Ernstsson/Davio/issues/94). **Revised 2026-11-16 by the owner:**
- one proposal per round, replacing the two-alternative trial;
- the separate reviewer restarted, ending the trial of 24 October, with nothing reaching the founder until it passes.

Both follow the #257 feedback-bar rounds (history below).

The founder approves the few core screens. The team owns the brief, exploration, internal review and recommendation before a screen reaches the founder. Each proposal continues the governing screen record and names the change it tests. The existing learner journey and approved screen behaviour remain authority.

## Brief and exploration

Start with:
- the Learner's one job in this part of the inhabited Target-language world;
- the governing journey and screen record, and the approved behaviour;
- the shared visual kit;
- **one open question** the still should answer.

Record visible controls and implied transitions as approved, provisional or unresolved, in the mockup's existing `QUESTION.md` or current spec. Name any conflict as a proposed change before drawing it. Use the current journey index and [screen consistency](../product-experience/screen-consistency.md); a rejected mockup is history, not a fresh brief.

Explore privately as needed. Each round brings **one proposal with the team's verdict**: a complete screen using the working kit that answers the open question, with everything else held still. Draw alternatives only when the founder asks for them. A round doesn't reopen settled screen behaviour or a selected direction. Preserve a selected direction while refining details or making it interactive, unless the founder reopens it.

## Internal gate

A reviewer who did not make the proposal checks it before founder review. The reviewer is a fresh agent and gets only:
- the image or images;
- the reference the maker used;
- today's screen.

The reviewer doesn't get the maker's rationale, and answers three questions:
- Is it clean and clear?
- Does it pass the hard failures below and the [learner-prototype](../../.agents/skills/learner-prototype/SKILL.md) review questions?
- Is it at least as good as the reference and today's screen?

**Hard failures.** The proposal:
- contradicts the governing screen record;
- loses Davio's learner job or inhabited-world identity;
- has a generic or incoherent visual direction;
- breaks the shared kit without a reason;
- has an unusable shorter-phone composition.

**Nothing reaches the founder until the reviewer passes it.** A review costs far less than the founder's look. After a failed review, the maker repairs and a fresh review looks again (the image, the reference and today's screen), for up to three reviews.
- If the proposal still fails after the third review, the founder gets the best candidate with the reviewer's remaining objections stated plainly in the handoff.
- Close calls about visual expression are design judgements: explain them with the verdict rather than turning taste into a checklist.

## Founder handoff and evidence

**What the founder gets:**
- One captioned image of the proposal at the intended phone size.
- A short decision brief: the Learner job, the open question, the team's verdict and trade-off, relevant unresolved behaviour, and what remains unproven.
- The shorter-phone stills, the rule map and the review notes stay with the artifact as evidence, available on request.

**What a still can establish:** visible hierarchy, composition, crops, wrapping, and apparent clearance at the rendered sizes.

**What it can't:** tap discoverability, transition or resume behaviour, actual hit areas, Android system insets, accessibility, or learning outcomes. Visual acceptance is separate from those later checks.

**Critique on request.** At the founder's request, a reviewer who neither made nor internally cleared the proposal gives an independent critique **before** the founder states a preference. It returns located findings, likely Learner consequences, evidence or a stated heuristic, and a recommendation. The founder keeps the visual decision.

## Why this handoff

The earlier commercial-fidelity demo let the founder taste-gate three moments and let the team own the screens between them. That did not stop weak, crowded, off-spec or one-off screens reaching the founder. A separate internal gate and one team verdict keep the quality responsibility with the team. The founder judges a screen, not a menu.

## History

- **2026-09-26 to 2026-11-16, two polished alternatives.** A one-screen trial asked for two complete alternatives differing on one visual question, with one recommendation, so the founder could judge a meaningful choice. On #257 the alternatives became a menu the owner could not tell apart. He asked for one proposal per round.
- **2026-10-24 to 2026-11-16, separate reviewer paused.** In an owner trial, the maker applied the gate to its own stills. In the #257 feedback-bar rounds, the maker passed and recommended its own stills, and the owner rejected most of them. The owner ended the trial and restarted the reviewer.
